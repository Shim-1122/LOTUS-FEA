What was checked

  • $verification_pass of $verification_total verification checks pass. They compare the solver's material, contact, hinge, cable, energy, restart and locking behaviour against analytical or independently computed results.
  • Skin and fat thickness are measured from the segmented surfaces at every skin vertex, with a median mesh deviation of $dev_skin mm (inner skin) and $dev_fat mm (inner fat).
  • Each socket petal's free body (lace, hinge, stabiliser and pad contact) balances to within $fb_resid% of the lace force.

What it does not tell us

  • No clinical validation has been done, so a converged run does not show grip, comfort or safety.
  • The limb is CAD built from approximations of an MRI scan; the bone labels (tibia, femur, patella, fibula) are heuristic.
  • $socket_note
  • Silicone and cushion properties are literature approximations (gel-liner silicone; 85A TPU with 8% gyroid infill), not tests of the actual parts. Tissue stiffness is the largest single uncertainty: in the synthetic sensitivity study, doubling or halving it changed peak displacement by −31% or +47%.
